## ✨ Project Overview

LUMORA brings daily planning and personal reflection together in one
calm, welcoming experience. It encourages progress through small,
intentional actions rather than trying to change everything at once.

The interface includes a personal overview, daily priorities, progress
indicators, a focus timer, and spaces for journaling and reflection.

## 🚀 Features

-   **Personal Overview** --- A central space to review the day and
    personal progress.
-   **Daily Priorities** --- Organize important tasks and track
    completed items.
-   **Goals & Habit Growth** --- Keep long-term dreams and everyday
    habits in view.
-   **Focus Studio** --- A 25-minute focus timer concept to encourage
    distraction-free work.
-   **Personal Journal** --- A dedicated space for check-ins and daily
    reflections.
-   **Future-Self Reflection** --- A prompt to consider the person you
    are working toward becoming.
-   **Lumi Assistant** --- An on-page feature presented as a way to
    untangle thoughts and identify a next step.
-   **Dark Appearance** --- A darker viewing option is presented in the
    interface.
-   **Calm, Motivational Design** --- Gentle language and a peaceful
    visual direction support the personal-growth theme.

## 🎯 Goals

LUMORA is designed to help users:

-   Turn big ambitions into small, manageable actions.
-   Create more intentional daily routines.
-   Keep priorities visible.
-   Make space for focus and reflection.
-   Notice progress without expecting perfection.

## 🧭 User Workflow

1.  Open the LUMORA overview.
2.  Review the day's priorities.
3.  Choose a small, meaningful task.
4.  Use the focus area to work on one thing at a time.
5.  Record thoughts or reflect on the day.
6.  Return regularly to build consistency over time.
